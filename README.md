# EarnVault

EarnVault is your personal career and compensation tracking app — a secure vault that records every rupee (or dollar) you’ve earned, every company you’ve worked at, and every benefit you’ve received. Whether you’re a salaried employee, a freelancer, or a seasoned professional, EarnVault helps you take complete control of your financial and professional history.

With EarnVault, you can chronologically document your salary slips, bonuses, provident fund (PF) contributions, tax payments, ESOPs/stocks, and total career experience. Upload important documents like offer letters, Form 16s, and payslips to create a digital archive of your career. Visualize your professional journey with powerful insights — track your salary growth, calculate total taxes paid, analyze PF accumulation, and understand how your compensation evolved across job switches.

Whether you’re preparing for a new job, applying for a loan, or just want to reflect on your career progression, EarnVault gives you an organized, insightful, and private way to manage it all.
